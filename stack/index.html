BEHIND THE SCENES

Our Stack

CanyoOS is the operating layer that ties these tools together. Each one has a clear job, and together they form the managed environment we run on your behalf.

// WHAT CANYOOS IS

CanyoOS is CANYO's internally operated management layer. It is not a public dashboard and it is not an off-the-shelf RMM. It is the system that coordinates monitoring, policy enforcement, automation, and escalation across every managed environment. The third-party tools below are the platforms CanyoOS orchestrates; CanyoOS itself is the glue that makes them act like one system.

OS

CanyoOS

COMING SOON

CANYO's internal management and orchestration layer.

Coordinates every other tool in this stack: monitoring, automation, policy enforcement, escalation, and record-keeping. It is how the pieces become one system.

If something else goes down: CanyoOS keeps running locally on managed devices, so endpoint checks, scripting, and data collection continue even if remote visibility is temporarily interrupted.

GZ

Guardz

LIVE TODAY

Threat detection and response across identity, email, cloud, endpoint, and data.

Guardz gives us a single pane for threats that cross multiple attack surfaces rather than forcing us to stitch together separate tools.

If it has an outage: Local SentinelOne EDR agents continue protecting endpoints. New threat intelligence, policy updates, and remote containment pause until service returns.

TS

Tailscale

LIVE TODAY

Zero-trust networking built on WireGuard.

Tailscale lets us reach your systems securely without opening public ports, removing a common external attack surface and letting your team work from anywhere.

If it has an outage: Already-established connections usually stay active because Tailscale uses peer-to-peer WireGuard. New connections are delayed until the coordination servers recover.

RS

Restic

LIVE TODAY

Encrypted, deduplicated, snapshot-based backups.

Restic creates versioned, verified snapshots efficiently and is vendor-neutral, giving us full control over schedules and restores.

If it has an outage: New backups stop running until the engine recovers. Existing snapshots remain safe and recoverable, and any local copies remain intact.

B2

Backblaze B2

LIVE TODAY

Immutable off-site object storage for Restic snapshots.

B2 gives us reliable, geographically separated storage with immutability options that protect backups from ransomware and local disasters.

If it has an outage: New backups cannot be written off-site, and restores from cloud storage are paused. Local copies and previously uploaded snapshots remain intact.

CA

CISO Assistant

LIVE TODAY

Governance, risk, and compliance tracking.

Maps controls to frameworks like HIPAA, ISO 27001, and SOC 2 so compliance evidence is tracked continuously rather than assembled in a scramble before an audit.

If it has an outage: Controls and documentation remain available from previous exports. New assessments and evidence collection pause until service returns.

// WHEN SOMETHING IN THE STACK GOES DOWN

No third-party tool is bulletproof, and we don't pretend otherwise. The design of our stack assumes individual components can have bad days. Local agents keep protecting the device, previously created backups remain safe, and established network sessions usually stay up. What typically pauses is our remote visibility, new policy distribution, or the ability to intervene manually until the service recovers. We monitor every layer and tell you honestly when something is affected.
