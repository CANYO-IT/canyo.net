Introducing CanyoOS

CanyoOS is the operating layer we've been building to run CANYO managed environments end-to-end. It's not a product you log into — it's the system our technicians use to watch, decide, authorize, preview, execute, verify, record, and escalate across every endpoint we protect.

The goal is simple: catch drift and disruption before it reaches your desk, while keeping a human in the loop for anything that matters.

We're still building. This blog is where we'll share progress, decisions, and lessons as CanyoOS moves from internal prototype to the backbone of every CANYO engagement.
