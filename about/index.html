// THE FOUNDER

The Person Behind CANYO

Samuel Glenn | Founder

I'm Samuel Glenn, founder of CANYO. I started CANYO after working for another local technology company and realizing that the way IT was being managed wasn't always how I would want it managed as a customer. I believe good IT is IT you don't notice — technology should be reliable, proactive, secure, and simple. Customers deserve transparency and a clear understanding of why things are being done, not a black box or another break/fix cycle. I approach technology with an open mind, listen before I speak, and believe there's usually more than one way to reach the right outcome.

I'm passionate about using modern technology, automation, and AI as tools — not crutches — to build systems that are capable, cost-effective, and built to grow. I spend a lot of time researching, testing, and understanding the tools I use because I believe if I can't explain something simply, I don't understand it well enough. My faith shapes the way I work as well. Colossians 3:23 reminds me to work wholeheartedly as though I'm working for the Lord, and I try to carry that same integrity into every interaction, including the ones nobody sees.

CANYO is named after my son, Canyon, who is one of the biggest reasons I strive to work hard, keep learning, and continue becoming a better person. I want CANYO to represent commitment, integrity, and the willingness to make a plan and work it. My goal isn't simply to build a successful IT company — it's to build something I'm proud of, something my customers can trust, and something that gives people a reason to want to know the person behind it.

// THE CANYO CORE PILLARS

How the Work Gets Done

01

Reversible-First Operations

Every change we make is designed to be safe and reversible. Major or risky changes are carefully vetted and never performed without explicit client approval.

02

Verification Over Assumption

We don't just assume fixes work. A ticket is closed only when results are verified against known-good system baselines. Transparency and reliability are our default.

03

Institutional Memory, Scripted Execution

We transform successful workflows into documentation and automated scripts — ensuring identical precision every time. Data governance is built in: we store only what's necessary to prove accountability, monitor performance, and guarantee backup readiness.
