Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Introduction

CANYO respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we may share it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you fill out a contact form or communicate with us. This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company name
  • Any message or details submitted through contact forms

We also automatically collect limited technical information necessary to operate and improve the website, such as:

  • Browser type and settings
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and interaction data
  • Approximate location derived from IP address

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide the services you request
  • Improve and maintain the website and user experience
  • Analyze site usage and trends
  • Protect the security and integrity of our systems

We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and Disclosure

We may share information with third-party service providers when necessary to operate the website or provide services, for example hosting providers, analytics vendors, and form processors. We require such providers to protect personal information and use it only for permitted purposes. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

Cookies and Analytics

The website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality and understand how visitors use the site. Analytics tools may collect aggregated usage data. Most browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through browser settings, though doing so may affect site functionality.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy and to meet legal or business obligations. When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

If a service agreement is terminated, CANYO may retain data related to the engagement for up to 30 days to allow for proper and safe transfer to the appropriate party. After that period, the data is securely deleted unless a separate written arrangement or legal obligation requires otherwise.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. However, no transmission or storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights and Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have submitted through the website. To make such a request or to ask questions about this policy, please contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page. Continued use of the website after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@canyo.net.
