Introducing

CanyoOS

Security that doesn't wait for you to notice.

CanyoOS is the always-on layer that watches every endpoint you manage, tells the difference between normal and wrong, and handles it — automatically, accountably, and in full view.

How it works ↓

CanyoOS is the operating layer behind every CANYO managed environment. Learn how it protects your business →

Most tools tell you something went wrong. Few tell you in time to matter.

Endpoints drift from their intended state constantly — a setting changes, an update lapses, something runs that shouldn't. By the time a ticket gets filed, a report gets read, or a dashboard gets refreshed, the window to act quietly has already closed. CanyoOS was built to close that gap.

The Canyo Loop

One continuous loop. Watching, deciding, acting — on repeat, forever.

Every endpoint under CanyoOS runs through the same disciplined cycle, thousands of times a day, without anyone having to ask it to.

Observe

Every device continuously reports what's actually true about it.

Decide

Observations are measured against what should be true.

Authorize

Anything consequential checks for permission first.

Act

Approved responses are carried out directly on the device.

Verify

CanyoOS confirms the fix actually held.

Escalate

When something is ambiguous, a human is brought in by design.

Every pass through the loop is recorded. Nothing CanyoOS does happens off the books.

Always watching

Nothing has to ask to be seen.

CanyoOS maintains a continuous, factual record of what's actually running across every machine it manages — software, processes, configuration, behavior. Not a snapshot taken once a day. Not a report generated on request. A living record, current to the minute, built from direct observation rather than guesswork.

That record is compared against known-good, independently sourced security baselines — so "normal" isn't a guess, it's a standard.

From signal to action

Finding the problem is easy. Knowing what to do about it — automatically, safely — is the hard part.

When something drifts out of line with policy, CanyoOS doesn't just flag it for later. It evaluates severity, checks what's actually safe to do, and — where it's authorized to — resolves it directly. Lower-risk issues are handled without interruption. Anything with real consequence is queued for a human decision, with full context attached, before anything changes.

Autonomous where it's safe. Accountable everywhere else.

Nothing happens quietly

Every action CanyoOS takes leaves a trail.

What was observed. What was decided. What was done, and why. Who approved it, if approval was needed. CanyoOS keeps a permanent, append-only account of its own behavior — so trusting the system never requires taking it on faith. If you ever need to know exactly what happened to a machine and when, the answer already exists.
